# Product Charter

Perfect Match Cloud is a proprietary, greenfield, SaaS-first, multi-tenant
quality management and compliance platform owned by Perfect Match Investments,
LLC. Development is local-first while the architecture remains cloud-deployable
and targeted to AWS for a future production environment.

The product is independent from Odoo and every ERP framework. Existing QMS
experience may inform functional domain knowledge, but no source, schema,
implementation, or framework-specific structure may be copied.

Perfect Match Cloud is intended eventually to provide equivalent or superior
functional capability to the existing QMS product through a new, independent
implementation. Functional parity does not exist at PMC-00 and is not claimed.

## Product principles

- Multi-tenancy and tenant isolation are architectural invariants.
- Auditability is a first-class requirement.
- Perfect Match owns the product framework and business logic.
- Security, maintainability, accessibility, and exceptional UX are release gates.
- Compliance workflows may be supported without claiming product certification.
