# Product naming and logo exploration (NOT an approved rebrand)

Status: exploratory only. The working product name remains Perfect Match QMS, independent SaaS (not Odoo). No production assets, packages, legal entities or customer-facing UI changed.

## Brand architecture
- Parent endorsement: by Perfect Match.
- Product: distinctive, pronounceable wordmark + original symbol.
- Descriptor: Quality Management Platform.
- Audience: US industrial, aerospace and defense suppliers; extensible to other sectors.
- Personality: precise, modern, reliable, accessible, confident; avoid generic checkmarks, ISO certification claims or borrowed competitor marks.

## Preliminary exclusions based on public search
Qualivra (existing software service and reported US application), Qualentra (MedTech quality/compliance), Tracevia (existing businesses), Qevanta, Qorvanta, Auvanta and Veyqora (existing technology/digital brands). This is not legal advice or comprehensive clearance.

## Visual system requirements
- Explore three concepts before final selection; use a small by Perfect Match endorsement.
- Check existing design tokens before choosing official colors.
- Test dark/light/monochrome, favicon 16 and 32 px, 48 px navigation symbol, horizontal lockup, mobile 320 px, desktop 1440 px and document cover.
- Require SVG source, exported PNG, accessible contrast, clearspace and minimum size guidance.
- Do not replace public assets until name, ownership and mark are approved.

## Approval gates
1. Select one or two candidates with customer pronunciation and recall testing.
2. Search USPTO exact and similar sounds/spellings, related goods/services, common-law uses and relevant international registries.
3. Verify domain and handles separately, including active and historical conflicts.
4. Obtain trademark clearance and confirm ownership and licensing.
5. Approve final logo before modifying production UI.
